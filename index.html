HOW THIS WORKS

No server.
No magic. Just honesty.

Zing is fully offline. Your quotes, preferences, data — all local. But it also means updates work a little differently than most apps.

NOW
Beta — APK + TestFlight
Android users download the APK directly. iOS users join via TestFlight. Updates require a redownload — but your saved creators, favourites and settings always stay safe.
EVERY 3 MONTHS
New quotes, new creators
Community-submitted quotes get verified and shipped. New creators added based on demand. Bug fixes and improvements included.
PLAY STORE + APP STORE
Auto-updates forever
Once on the stores, updates happen automatically. No redownloads. No manual steps. The community announcement comes first — no one is ever surprised.
✅

Your data is always safe

Updating Zing never wipes your saved creators, favourites, font choice, or frequency settings. The new version just adds more quotes on top.

📢

You'll always know before it drops

Every update gets announced on social media before it ships. What's new, what changed, which creators were added — all transparent.

🔌

Why no server?

Because servers cost money, collect data, and break. Zing works offline. Always. No Wi-Fi needed, no account, no tracking. The tradeoff is manual updates during beta — worth it.

iOS USERS

iPhone? We got you.

Zing is available on iOS through Apple's official beta platform — TestFlight. No App Store needed. No waiting. Just 3 steps.

01

Download TestFlight from the App Store — it's Apple's official beta testing app. Free.

02

Tap the iOS TestFlight button at the top of this page. It opens the Zing invite link.

03

Hit Accept and install. Zing is on your iPhone. Done.

TestFlight builds expire after 90 days — but we update every 3 months anyway. Once Zing hits the App Store, TestFlight users migrate automatically.
